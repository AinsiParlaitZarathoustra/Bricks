# Get unstuck

1. Write down what you tried and what each attempt showed.
2. List the assumptions behind those attempts; check the doubtful ones against the code,
   the documentation or a small experiment.
3. Look for a simpler approach, or a smaller step that gives new information.
4. If the obstacle is a missing piece of information or a decision that belongs to the
   user, stop and ask a precise question instead of guessing.
5. Continue with the new approach, and say what changed in your plan.
